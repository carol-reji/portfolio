Blue Ghost lunar lander composite tooling

HERO — DETOOLER + OVEN-SAFE TRANSPORT CART
PLY SCHEDULE MARKUP
CMM FLATNESS MAP

A flight-critical structural beam kept coming off the tool bridged and out of flat. I rewrote the ply schedule — tapered inner plies, staggered overlaps, targeted intermediate debulks — and specified a crane-operable, oven-safe cart to detool a 6,500 lbf part without inducing warp. Then I wrote the work instruction so the technicians could run it without me.
